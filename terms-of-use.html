2. Pediatric Therapy Scope of Practice

Suja's Child Development Centre provides specialized pediatric rehabilitative and developmental therapies, including:

  • Occupational Therapy & Sensory Integration (OT & SI)
  • Speech & Language Therapy
  • Early Intervention & Neuro Developmental Therapy (NDT)
  • Behavioural Modification & Autism Spectrum Support
  • Special Education, Handwriting & School Readiness Programs
  • Pediatric Physiotherapy & Gross Motor Rehabilitation

All therapy services are planned, evaluated, and administered under the professional leadership of certified therapists led by Dr. J. Jansirani, B.O.T..

4. Appointments, Booking & Consultations

Initial consultations and recurring therapy sessions may be scheduled through our website consultation forms, WhatsApp, or phone. A consultation booking request becomes confirmed only after our administrative desk confirms the assigned therapist, date, and time slot.

5. Attendance, Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Consistent attendance is essential for therapeutic progress and neuroplasticity reinforcement in children. We request parents and guardians to respect the following clinical scheduling rules:

  • Notice for Rescheduling: Please provide at least 24 hours advance notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a confirmed therapy session.
  • Punctuality: Sessions begin promptly at the allocated time. Late arrivals may result in shortened session duration to avoid impacting subsequent patients.
  • Sickness & Infections: If your child displays active fever, contagious rash, productive cough, or viral symptoms, please notify us to reschedule the session for the safety of other children in our sensory gym.

6. Parental / Guardian Presence & Supervision

Parents or designated legal guardians must remain on the clinic premises during therapy sessions unless explicitly guided otherwise by the treating therapist for specific sitting tolerance or school readiness routines. SCDC emphasizes parent education and home program continuity to ensure maximum developmental outcomes.

7. Individualized Milestones & Progress Expectations

Every child possesses a distinct neurodevelopmental timeline. While our evidence-based clinical methodologies have successfully guided hundreds of children toward independence, developmental milestones and rate of progress vary per individual child based on neurological profile, baseline abilities, consistency of attendance, and home program reinforcement.
