Occupational Therapy & Sensory Integration

Active sensory gym therapies, motor planning, and fine motor exercises to build physical confidence, focus, and daily independence.

  • Sensory Integration Therapy: Active sensory gym sessions helping children regulate sensory input, improve focus, and build spatial balance.
  • Occupational & Fine Motor Practice: Targeted exercises for hand strength, pencil grip, handwriting practice, and daily self-care routines.
  • Sitting Tolerance & School Readiness: Structured play routines designed to build sitting duration, attention span, and classroom readiness.

Speech & Language Therapy

Interactive speech training to enhance vocabulary, speech clarity, oral motor control, and social communication confidence.

  • Speech Training & Articulation: Targeted exercises to improve sound production, word clarity, and expressive language skills.
  • Oral Stimulation & Motor Therapy: Specialized oral stimulation techniques to support feeding, swallowing, and speech muscle coordination.
  • Social Communication & Fluency: Interactive sessions to build conversational skills, listening comprehension, and social confidence.
