Our Core Philosophy

Support • Guide • Build Skills • Grow

At Suja's Child Development Centre (SCDC) in Perumbakkam, Chennai, we believe every child has unique strengths and potential. Led by Dr. J. Jansirani, B.O.T. (Bachelor of Occupational Therapy), our multidisciplinary centre combines evidence-based therapies, sensory-rich play environments, and individualized family-centered care.

Whether your child is facing developmental milestones delays, sensory sensitivities, speech clarity challenges, or social learning difficulties, our team creates a compassionate roadmap for their growth, independence, and happiness.

Core Speciality

Occupational Therapy & Sensory Integration

Active sensory gym therapies, motor planning, and fine motor exercises to build physical confidence, focus, and daily independence.

● Sensory Integration Therapy

Active sensory gym sessions helping children with Sensory Processing Disorder regulate sensory input, improve focus, and build spatial balance.

● Occupational & Fine Motor Practice

Targeted exercises for hand strength, pencil grip, handwriting practice, and daily self-care routines.

● Sitting Tolerance & School Readiness

Structured play routines designed to build sitting duration, attention span, and classroom readiness.

Communication & Fluency

Speech & Language Therapy

Interactive speech training to enhance vocabulary, speech clarity, oral motor control, and social communication confidence.

● Speech Training & Articulation

Targeted exercises to address speech disorders, improve sound production, word clarity, and expressive language skills.

● Oral Stimulation & Motor Therapy

Specialized oral stimulation techniques to support feeding, swallowing, and speech muscle coordination.

● Social Communication & Fluency

Interactive sessions to build conversational skills, listening comprehension, and social confidence.

Early Childhood Support (0–6 Years)

Early Intervention & Neuro Developmental Therapy

Timely, nurturing early intervention programmes and neuro-developmental support to unlock foundational milestones during a child's most critical growth years.

● Early Intervention Programme

Holistic milestone tracking and play-based stimulation for infants and toddlers showing early developmental delays.

● Neuro Developmental Therapy (NDT)

Specialized therapeutic handling and movement facilitation for children with neurological conditions and Cerebral Palsy.

● Cognitive & Play-Based Skill Building

Guided activities rooted in our "Support • Guide • Build Skills • Grow" philosophy to foster curiosity and independence.

Behavior & Academic Learning

Behavioural Modification & Special Education

Compassionate, individualized behavioral strategies and remedial academic support for children with Autism, ADHD, and learning disabilities.

● Autism (ASD) & ADHD Care

Individualized therapy plans tailored to autism spectrum milestones, eye contact, hyperactivity management, and social engagement.

● Behavioural Modification Therapy

Nurturing, evidence-based techniques to address behavioral disorders, tantrums, impulsivity, and emotional regulation.

● Special Education & Learning Support

Customized educational strategies for children with Learning Disabilities, Intellectual Disability, and reading/writing difficulties.

Signs to Reach Us

  • Delay in Speech or Unclear Speech
  • Delay in Developmental Milestones
  • Lack of Eye Contact or Social Response
  • Hyperactivity & Restlessness
  • Frequent Tantrums or Behavioral Challenges
  • Difficulty with Sensory Inputs (Sounds, Textures)
  • Poor Sitting Tolerance or Attention Span
  • Reading, Writing & Learning Difficulties
