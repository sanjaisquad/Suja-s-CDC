Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026 • Effective Date: February 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment to Patient Confidentiality

At Suja's Child Development Centre (SCDC), led by Dr. J. Jansirani, B.O.T., we are committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our young patients and their families. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, retention, and protection of personal and medical information collected through our clinic and our website (sujaschilddevelopmentcenter.in).

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality, customized therapeutic interventions, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Parent / Guardian Details: Full names, residential address, telephone numbers, email addresses, and relationship to the child.
  • Child's Personal Information: Child's full name, date of birth, age, school/grade, and pediatrician referrals.
  • Developmental & Medical History: Birth history, milestone timelines, previous therapeutic evaluations, behavioral observations, sensory profiles, speech-language assessments, and clinical diagnosis (e.g., ASD, ADHD, GDD, Cerebral Palsy).
  • Inquiry & Appointment Data: Information submitted through our consultation booking forms, WhatsApp chat, and telephone inquiries.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

All information collected is strictly utilized for clinical care and administrative management, including:

  • Formulating individualized occupational therapy, speech therapy, and early intervention plans.
  • Conducting baseline milestone evaluations and tracking developmental progress over time.
  • Scheduling, confirming, and coordinating clinical appointments.
  • Direct therapeutic communication with parents, guardians, and referring medical specialists.
  • Compliance with health record obligations under applicable Indian medical guidelines.

4. Child & Minor Privacy Protections

As a pediatric centre, we recognize the supreme importance of protecting minors' privacy. We never collect personal information directly from children without explicit, documented parental or legal guardian consent. Parents retain the unconditional right to review, update, or request copies of their child's therapeutic documentation at any time.

5. Clinical Media & Video Assessment Policy

During therapeutic evaluations or therapy sessions, therapists may record clinical photographs or brief video clips to assess gait, fine motor grip, sensory response, or speech articulation. Such media is captured only with prior explicit parental consent, is stored within encrypted clinical records, and is never published or utilized for promotional marketing without separate, written authorization from parents.

6. Data Security & Confidentiality

We maintain strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of confidential health records. Clinical files are stored securely and accessible only by qualified therapists directly involved in your child's care.

7. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing

Suja's Child Development Centre never sells, rents, or commercializes your personal or clinical data. We only share records with external medical doctors, pediatricians, or school educators upon the explicit written request or signed consent of the parent or legal guardian, or when mandated by law.

8. Website Analytics & Cookies

Our website utilizes basic technical cookies and standard privacy-preserving web analytics to understand visitor navigation and optimize user experience. No sensitive clinical data is ever stored in browser cookies.

9. Contact Our Data & Privacy Officer
